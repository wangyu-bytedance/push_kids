# DREV04 — 六种教育主题图像生成提示词

- 生成方式：内置 image_gen（ui-mockup），每个方向一次独立生成。
- 用途：设置页主题概念探索，非已批准的可实施视觉基线。
- 用户要求：教育属性更强，与前六版不同；拓展背景、图标、插画和布局。
- 图片不使用真实儿童、真实数据或现有品牌角色。

## A · 成长花园

纸雕花园、嫩芽角色、叶片与花盆图标

```text
Use case: ui-mockup.
Create ONE polished high-fidelity mobile WeChat Mini Program UI screenshot for a Chinese parent-facing primary-school learning organizer called 知芽. A fresh imaginative education-oriented art direction, sophisticated and charming for parents and children aged 6–12, never a generic SaaS settings screen, never a simple recolor.
Output a single flat front-facing edge-to-edge portrait screen, approximately 9:19.5 aspect ratio, high resolution. No perspective, physical phone mockup, external frame, presentation caption, or watermark. The screen itself is the entire image. Render all UI sharply and coherently with excellent simplified Chinese typography and readable contrast.
Same functional content in all variants; you can reshape section geometry, background, decorative illustrations, icon language, typography and layout within this screen. Top compact iOS status bar 9:41; standard WeChat mini nav with sprout brand “知芽” and menu capsule at right. Page title “设置”, secondary line “小禾的学习与日常”, small child profile selector “小禾”.
After the illustrated header, include the following EXACT Chinese content, in this order, retaining clear information hierarchy:
“在学科目” with three subject controls “语文” “数学” “英语” and a plus.
Section “课外活动” contains TWO rows:
“围棋” / “每周日 14:00–16:00”
“游泳” / “每周日 17:00–18:00”
and one full-width “＋ 添加课外活动” button.
Section “每日任务” contains TWO rows:
“阅读” / “每天 20 分钟”
“围棋” / “每天 30 分钟”
and one full-width “＋ 添加每日任务” button.
Bottom fixed navigation: five matching custom pictorial icons and Chinese labels “今日” “日程” “记录” “报表” “设置”. Settings selected. Equal navigation size, do not make the middle camera enormous. iPhone home indicator below.
The two add buttons MUST use exactly the same visual style, same surface and color within this variant. Each row has a clearly relevant custom designed icon and a subtle disclosure cue. All four rows and both add buttons fully visible above the tab bar. Keep comfortable touch sizes, spacious readable body text. The illustrated header and decorative environment together occupy roughly 20–25% of the screen; let the actual content have calm solid backgrounds. Artwork may extend along outer margins but never behind vital text.
No real children, no real personal data, no learning grades, no mastery scores, no progress claims, no extra gamification systems, no product features invented. This is concept art of the settings page, not a game.
ART DIRECTION FOR THIS IMAGE:
A growing garden made of finely crafted layered cut paper, paper relief, and crisp contemporary editorial illustration. The HEADER becomes a tiny sunlit learning garden: sculptural paper leaves, a sprouting seed, a small smiling sprout character reading an open book, a watering can and a peach-colored sun. Pale sky with organic layered foliage at the extreme edges. Use a fresh leaf green / warm light cream / persimmon / butter-yellow palette with strong charcoal text, not muted vintage sage. Settings sections resemble delicately scalloped paper labels nested in a garden, with stems and small flowers guiding the vertical flow outside the content. Custom tactile paper-cut icons: a tiny Go board, swimming ring with wave, open book, Go stones. Small leaves integrated into the two matching broad leaf-green add buttons, shaped as friendly rounded garden markers; text stays straight and legible. Subject controls as clean little seed-packet tabs. Botanical bottom-nav icons drawn in the same disciplined paper language. Beautiful art-directed backgrounds and shadows between paper layers, generous negative space. NOT plain rectangular white cards on flat green. Distinct garden storytelling with contemporary polish.
```
